Try on Neuryo

Face the camera and the headset appears on your head, true to size. Turn your head up to about three-quarters; a full side view loses the face.

The video stays on this device. Face tracking runs in your browser (Google MediaPipe); nothing is uploaded.
Use the head guide to line up the fit, then hide it.
