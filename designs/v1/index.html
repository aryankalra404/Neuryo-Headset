Added build details
  • Seal the solder joint; choose sintered Ag/AgCl. In saline, sintered Ag/AgCl had the most stable potential and lowest noise; tin (the main metal in solder) corroded and had noise 1–2 orders of magnitude higher. Alves, Fiedler & Fonseca, Coatings 2026
  • Hole in the electrode for saline. Standard EEG cups have “a hole in the top for electrolyte injection.” Electrode Store, GH series
  • Blot excess saline. Electrolyte spreading between electrodes forms bridges that shrink the signal; remove excess before recording. Greischar et al., Clin. Neurophysiol. 2004
  • Sliding pod mounts. 10–20 sites are set at 10% and 20% steps of each person’s own nasion–inion distance, so fixed spacing can’t fit every head. 10–20 system (after Jasper 1958)
Ideas for the team (not in the model)
  • Smaller front module: ESP32-S3-MINI-1 is 15.4 × 20.5 × 2.4 mm vs 18 × 25.5 × 3.1 mm, but has 2 MB PSRAM instead of 8 MB. Espressif datasheet
  • Heart sensor pressure: PPG amplitude rises steadily with contact pressure, so keep strap pressure consistent and test it. Bangari et al., SPIE 2026
