On intact skin the headset is a CDC “noncritical” item: a low-level disinfectant wipe between patients (R18). Everything that holds gel or touches skin directly is single-use (D32).

What it is
A 5-minute brain and heart recording taken at each treatment visit; tracks change in the same patient. Monitoring aid, not diagnosis (D01, D31)
First use
Depression treatment courses: baseline, day 7, every follow-up. Hospital psychiatry and rTMS clinics first
On the head
7 channels · Fp1, Fp2, Fz, Cz, Pz, M2, eye (VEOG)
EEG chip
ADS1299, 8 channels · ch 7 Cz · ch 8 ECG (test kit only)
Reference / bias
M1 left mastoid on SRB2 (D25) · bias at Fpz (centre pad of the strip)
Gain, sampling
12 (±375 mV range, D29) · 250 samples/s · 24-bit · noise target < 6 µVpp
Forehead
Single-use 3-pad hydrogel strip, Ag/AgCl (Fp1, Fpz, Fp2), placed on the skin with a nasion template (centre pad at 10% of nasion–inion); it runs on to both temple pads (thin arms with an S-bend), its left end plugging into a socket under the left temple pad and its right end tucked under the right pad, so it reads as one line joining the arms. One size: pads about 16 mm, centres 27 mm apart (25.0–29.0 mm needed on 500–580 mm heads). MVP: three separate 22 × 22 mm Ag/AgCl snap pads stuck through the template card, with three snap leads to the board. Nothing presses on them: the frame touches the forehead nowhere, and the temple pads sit beside and above the strip
In hair
Semi-dry pods at Fz, Cz, Pz: a 9 mm sealed pod body (cup + epoxied lid, 4 mm stud) with a printed cap the midline strap runs through; snap lead out of the cap’s side · single-use foam tip cartridge that clicks onto each pod, sticking out 2–3 mm, wetted to a set weight (damp, not soaked) · no reservoir: 2–4 drops (0.1–0.2 ml) of 0.9% saline through the cap’s side fill port, whose channel runs into the lid’s centre hole, only when the contact check asks, so saline goes straight into the cup and never onto the lid, snap or lead
Mastoids, eye
Adhesive gel Ag/AgCl pads (M1, M2) · small hydrogel pad 20 mm below the left eye
Heart
MAX30102 earlobe clip · pulse-rate variability with medication class (D09, D12, D22)
Motion
BMI270 IMU on the frame (X29)
Main board
Rev B, 4-layer, about 70 × 50 mm, in the left pod above the ear, about 20.5 mm off the skin (fit check on this head) · analog half copied from Low-Cost-EEG, a built and tested open ADS1299 + ESP32-S3 board (GPL-3.0) · Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 with USB-C, charger and Bluetooth · flex antenna on the pod’s outer face, away from the steel arch
Fit
One size for 500–580 mm circumference and 300–390 mm nasion–inion (D19): a spring-steel arch over Cz with a slider each side (about ±15 mm per side, estimate), short arms to soft temple pads, and a rear band with a hook under the occiput tightened by a dial · the arch lies on the T7–Cz–T8 line, so the ears place Cz; check against the size tape’s 50% mark · one elastic midline strap from a front Y clip (legs back along the hair to each pod) over Fz, Cz and Pz to the rear band; the Fz–Cz–Pz section is non-stretch webbing and Fz and Pz pin into the patient’s size hole (1–9, 0.2 × nasion–inion either side of Cz), saved per patient; elastic only at the ends
Pods in hair
v5.1: no springs or carriages. The elastic midline strap runs over a printed cap on each pod and, being under tension, pushes each pod straight into the scalp with tension × the angle it turns over that pod. Per newton of tension that is about 0.82–0.99 N on each pod across 300–390 mm nasion–inion and ±8% head shape (tools/strap_force.py), so at about 1.4 N of tension every pod gets about 1.1–1.4 N on any head. The Cz cap has a 12 mm riser so Cz gets the same share. The elastic ends carry a stretch mark for the tension; the middle is non-stretch webbing, so the pods’ positions do not move with it
Materials
Wipe-clean pods, pod caps and arm covers (PETG prints for the prototype; PC/ABS in the product) · spring-steel arch (from over-ear headphones for the mock-up) · 20 mm elastic midline strap with printed size marks · soft TPU / silicone pads at the temples, the rear hook and inside the pods · single-use skin contacts (D32, D34)
Power
1000 mAh LiPo, battery only while worn · about 8–10 h estimated (v4.5: the XIAO ESP32-S3 alone draws about 85 mA with Bluetooth active) · charged through the XIAO’s USB-C, which blocks recording
Session
About 12 min chair time: under 5 min setup, 5 min recording (EC 2 · EO 1 · EC 2), sitting upright with no headrest behind the head (the rear hook and dial are there), contact checks between blocks (D27, D28)
Output
One-page trend report with personal baseline, change bands, medication class and PHQ-9 on the same timeline (D05)
Regulatory
India: medical device (D23), plan for CDSCO Class B, budget for C · IEC 60601-1, 60601-1-2, 80601-2-26, 62304, ISO 10993

New in v4
  • Session-based, not continuous (D31). NICE reviews at 2 and 4 weeks; early clinical improvement already has NPV 82–100% but PPV 19–60%, so the day-7 EEG must add to it (D33). NICE NG222 · Szegedi 2009
  • Cz (under test). Week-1 central cordance change predicted rTMS outcome (n=18); prefrontal cordance fell in all rTMS responders in a double-blind trial; not replicated in a third study. Hunter 2018 · Bares 2015 · Bailey 2019
  • Single-use forehead strip. A cleared home forehead EEG device attaches with a self-adhesive disposable patch at Fp1/Fp2; patients with insomnia or depression self-applied forehead EEG at home. FDA K231546 · Levendowski 2017
  • Rigid frame, spring at each pod (D34). Headsets with electrodes in hair hold them with a rigid frame and a spring per electrode: OpenBCI Ultracortex (3D-printable, spring-loaded nodes), Wearable Sensing DSI-24 (spring-loaded arms and pods, about 600 g), Zeto (FDA-cleared semi-rigid, flexible PA12 headset with flexible electrode legs, for outpatient clinics and home). Bitbrain's semi-dry system uses a fabric cap that is washed after every session instead. OpenBCI · DSI-24 · FDA K260455 · Bitbrain
  • Arch frame (v5). Headsets put electronics behind or above the ears (Muse, Emotiv EPOC) or on a hub with springy arms (Wearable Sensing DSI-7: 52–62 cm adult; Cognionics patent). DSI-7 · US 10,765,363 · Muse teardown · Emotiv EPOC X
  • Wipe-clean, no fabric (D32, D34). Items on intact skin need low-level disinfection; reusable cloths are often not cleaned well. CDC
  • Head-ECG test. ECG reaches head sites (neck and jaw best, forehead worst); mastoids untested, so the earlobe clip stays until Study 1. von Rosenberg 2017
  • BMI270 IMU. Current wearable IMU, 685 µA at full rate. Bosch Sensortec
  • Closest competitor. Universal Brain UB ERP: 7 dry channels, about 5 minutes, ERP tasks, now developing treatment-response markers. Universal Brain
Why these sites
  • Fp1 and Fp2 (D13). A week-1 drop in prefrontal theta predicted antidepressant response, including in an independent Ottawa group (AUC 0.85, n=47). Am J Psychiatry 2006 · Brunovsky 2019 · de la Salle 2020 · Srivastava 2026 meta-analysis
  • Pz for P300 (D08). The FDA-cleared UB ERP system records Fp1, Fp2, F3, F4, Fz, Cz, Pz with a gel EOG. FDA K253767
  • Eye channel (D26). EOG regression needs a reference channel; ICA can’t run on few channels. Jiang 2019
Reference and bias
  • Mastoid bone (review). The skin around the ear “including the mastoid bone” is described as hair free; adhesive pads fail through hair. Bleichner 2017 · Krishnan 2019
  • No hardware-linked mastoids (D06, D25). Linking ears online bridges the hemispheres; average and REST need far more channels. Yao 2019
  • M2 as a heart-artifact reference (judgement). ECG is best seen between the two ears. Anderer 1999
Chip, gain and wiring
  • Gain 12 (D29). Input range is ±VREF/gain: ±187.5 mV at 24, ±375 mV at 12; noise 0.16 vs 0.14 µVrms. Electrode offset tolerance is ±300 mV. TI ADS1299 datasheet · Xu 2017
  • Reference on SRB2. SRB2 is set per channel (P input); SRB1 ties every N input together, so no channel could run differential. OpenBCI Cyton SDK
  • Battery only, ±2.5 V (judgement). Cyton runs from a battery only and regulates +2.5 V and −2.5 V. OpenBCI Cyton specs
  • Lead-off between blocks (D27). AC lead-off runs at 7.8 or 31.2 Hz, inside the EEG band. TI ADS1299 datasheet
Electrodes
  • No dry contact at theta sites (D30). Dry electrodes raised delta and theta against gel and lost more data (10.5% vs 3.9%); high impedance in warm rooms added slow noise. Heijs 2021 · Hinrichs 2020 · Kappenman 2010
  • Paste on the forehead (review). Fp1 to Fpz is 26.9 mm on this head; the budget’s 32 × 25 mm pads would touch. Neo Gel pad size
  • Semi-dry in hair. Under 40 kΩ for 5 h; PVA/PAM 18.2 kΩ on hair. Plain sponges rose in impedance after about 20 min without rewetting (L37); v4.3 records for 5 min, so a pre-wetted tip plus top-up drops on demand replaces the reservoir (D21: a rewetting method is kept). Hua 2019 · Li 2024
  • Mixing contact types is fine. UB ERP pairs dry EEG with a gel EOG; with a high-impedance amplifier, unequal contacts mainly add mains hum. FDA K253767 · Ferree 2001
  • Ag/AgCl (D07). Lowest drift and noise. Alves 2026
Heart sensor and fit
  • Earlobe PPG (D22). Earlobe PRV vs ECG HRV r > 0.92 at rest. Vescio 2018 · Mejía-Mejía 2020
  • ECG in the test kit (D09). PPG must be checked against ECG on our own sensor and site before any HRV claim.
  • Fitted frame (D19). Re-placement 3.0 mm with a fitted cap vs 9.8 mm with straps. Hunold 2020 · Indian adult head circumference 499–578 mm. Shah 2018
  • Size range (v4.4). Adult nasion–inion typically 32–38 cm. Campbell 2009 · 80 adult dry skulls 305–356 mm. Rashid 2025
  • Mechanics check (v5). Pod forces, hold-down, pivot, bar stiffness and fit range: Neuryo headset concept study (7 Nov 2026). Head-surface angles and the pod fit measured on this head model; weights and forces are estimates.
  • Main board (v4.5). ADS1299 + ESP32-S3 board built and tested (ECG, EMG, alpha, SSVEP, P300), GPL-3.0. Low-Cost-EEG · rebuilt, alpha test. diy-eeg-mac
  • Microcontroller (v4.5). XIAO ESP32-S3: 21 × 17.8 mm, LiPo charger, reset and boot buttons, U.FL antenna, about 85 mA with Bluetooth active. Seeed · ESP32-S3 has two I²C controllers. Espressif
Mannequin
  • Head, ears, neck. MakeHuman base mesh (CC0), 540 mm head circumference. MakeHuman
  • M1 and M2 measured on this head by tools/mastoid.py: halfway between ear-canal and earlobe height, 16 mm behind the back edge of the ear so a 25 mm pad clears it.
  • Electrode sizes. FRI does not publish TDE-211/212 dimensions; disc, stud and socket sizes are model estimates. Measure the parts and update DIM at the top of the page script.
Still to prove on this hardware
  • Contact on Indian hair, oiled and washed, warm and cool rooms: impedance every 15 min for an hour, semi-dry vs paste vs dry spike.
  • Forehead strip vs paste; same-day and 1-week retest against the VIT wet cap (theta and alpha at Fp1, Fp2, Fz).
  • Earlobe PPG, forehead PPG and the M2 minus M1 head-ECG vs chest ECG on channel 8. Cz kept only if the full-cap subset test says it adds.
  • Setup under 5 minutes; comfort at 0, 30 and 60 minutes. Noise floor with shorted inputs (< 6 µVpp).
