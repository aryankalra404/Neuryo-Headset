## What the product is
A 5-minute brain and heart recording taken in clinic at baseline, day 7 and every follow-up visit of a depression treatment course. It tracks change in the same patient against their own baseline and shows it next to PHQ-9 and the medication class. It is a monitoring aid, not a diagnosis (D01, D31). First customers: hospital psychiatry departments and rTMS clinics.

Why sessions and not continuous wear (D31): the early-change evidence is all session-based; clinicians do not want streams of passive data; and head-worn devices lose adherence fast when worn at home.

## What changed from v3, and why
| Change | v3 | v4 | Why | How sure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Use case finalised | Tracks change | 12-min session (5 min recording) at baseline, day 7, every visit | Early-change evidence is session-based; clinicians are overloaded by passive data; head wearables lose adherence (D31) | High |
| Cz on channel 7 | Ch 7 ECG, ch 8 free | Ch 7 Cz, ch 8 ECG | Central cordance change predicted rTMS outcome (K102); P300 drop in depression largest at Cz (M10). Kept only if Study 1 says it adds | Low to medium |
| Single-use forehead strip | Snap + Ten20 paste | 3-pad hydrogel strip | No paste, no cleanup; a cleared forehead EEG device uses a disposable patch (C69, D32). Paste stays as the fallback | Medium |
| Eye pad | Snap + paste, ring | Small hydrogel pad | Single-use and quicker to place (D32) | Engineering |
| Pod caps | Bare cups on the arc | Wipe-clean caps, top refill port | Product look, protects the cup, wipes clean between patients (R18) | Design |
| Knit cover | None | Removable, washable sleeve | The soft look without a permanent fabric layer that cannot be disinfected (R18, D32) | Design |
| Fit dial and sizes | Fitted frame | S/M/L frames + rear dial, reading saved | Same fit every visit: re-placement 3.0 mm with a fitted cap vs 9.8 mm with straps (P186, D19) | High |
| Brow module | 45° wide, 19 mm thick | 52° wide, 15 mm thick | Lower profile, load spread across the brow. Shape is still a model estimate | Design |
| Battery | 2000 mAh | 1000 mAh | About 14 h at an estimated 0.25 W covers a clinic day, with less weight at the back | Judgement |
| Motion sensor | MPU-6050 | BMI270 | A current wearable IMU; the MPU-6050 lifecycle could not be confirmed (X29) | Engineering |
| Forehead heart window | None | Test kit only | Compare forehead and earlobe PPG against ECG before choosing (X26, X27, D22) | High |
| Head-ECG check | Not planned | M2 minus M1 vs chest ECG | If it matches, v5 can drop the earlobe clip (X28) | Judgement |

## Cleaning model (D32, R18)
- **Single-use, binned after every session:** forehead strip, M1/M2 pads, eye pad, three foam tips.
- **Wiped between patients (low-level disinfection, noncritical item):** shell, pod caps, earlobe clip, fit dial, bands.
- **Washed at the end of the day:** knit cover.

Turn on **Display → Single-use vs reusable** (or press U) to see this on the model.

The **Session walkthrough** steps through one clinic session on the model in 9 steps: check in, prep the skin, fit the frame, click on the strip, wet the pods, pads and heart clip, contact check, record, remove and clean. Arrow keys move between steps; Esc exits.
