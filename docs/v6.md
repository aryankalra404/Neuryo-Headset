# v6 · head ring + v4 midline (8 Nov 2026; v6.2, 10 Nov; v6.3, 11 Nov)

Live: https://aryankalra404.github.io/Neuryo-Headset/designs/v6/

**What it is:** v4's midline band and spring carriages on a lighter frame. Electronics are unchanged from v4.5 / v5. The headset has 6 channels on the head (Fp1, Fp2, Fz, Pz, M2, eye; M1 reference, Fpz bias). Since v6.3 there is no Cz channel in the product or the test kit (decision D36 in Neuryo-Research), and the band has no Cz window. See [v6.3](#v63-11-nov-no-cz-two-window-band).

## Why (changes from v4 and v5)
- **Midline on the head, not over it.** A band lying on the head sets each pod's height from the scalp, so the spring only takes up hair. v5's rigid spine stood 29 mm off the head; the gap changed with head shape, which forced a long spring, wedge-cut carriages and an offset arch. Same principle as Zeto's FDA-cleared headset (semi-rigid bands with fixed Fz, Cz, Pz and a spring per electrode: Neuryo-Research C72, C80) and OpenBCI's spring holders (C71, C77). A light strip over the top on a headband was the most comfortable layout in Hairston 2014 (C82).
- **Small front anchor instead of the brow module.** The board is now above the left ear, so the front only has to hold the band's end and set its 0 mm mark over Fpz.
- **Head ring instead of the arch.** A band that lies on the head has to be held at both ends; the arch could not hold its front end. The front band and anchor do.
- **Cam lock above the dial.** v4 clamped the tail on the battery pod at the back; the battery is now above the right ear.

## Checks
- Model: windows and lining are real openings and the lead leaves each carriage through a slot. A collision check of the frame, band, anchor, carriages, pods and leads on the page (8 Nov) found no parts passing through each other; the only overlaps left are leads plugging into their snap heads and the pods.
- Not yet measured: the harness range (PN521), the front band's fit on small and large foreheads, the anchor size, spring force (kitchen scale). Patent: Zeto US 11,166,673 is probably still in force; check its claims before a product.

A printed latch in the right shoe clicks into a notch on the rail's outer edge | The spring pushes the pod down and the carriage up with the same 1–3 N. **Why Cz moved to the test kit (v6.2; superseded by v6.3, which drops Cz altogether).** Its two reasons do not apply to the session as planned: the P300 evidence (M10, C03) needs a task and the 5-minute session is rest only, and central cordance (K102, n=18) cannot be claimed from a 6 to 8 channel montage (D18). The 19+ channel wet-cap study answers whether Cz adds anything, and it does that without a Cz pod on the headset. Dropping it removes one contact that has to pass before a session counts (D11) and one pod to build. v6.2 kept the Cz window so the pod could go back in. v6.3 removes the window too (D36): Cz returns only if an oddball task is added to the session or the subset test shows it adds, and either way that means a reprinted band.

**Kept as it was: two side pods of the same size.** The review suggested moving the battery into the left pod. That was wrong on balance: the board and the cell weigh about the same, so one each side balances the headset, and matching shells look right. Keep both. Twist the battery's two wires together along the rear band, since they carry the radio's current pulses next to the M2 lead.

**Checks (10 Nov, headless render).** No page errors in Product or Test kit mode. `tools/collide.js`: no new overlaps apart from each shoe meeting its own foot (one print) and the thicker rails meeting the band they are part of. Second pass the same day: the session walkthrough (9 steps), pod guide (6 steps, three tips) and board guide (8 steps) were stepped through with no page errors; their text now says two hair pods (two tip cartridges, no Cz highlight). The clips, strap scale and trimmed pads add no overlaps with the leads, band or head ring. The re-exported model loads in `tryon.html` and shows Fz and Pz only (checked with a set head pose, not a live camera).

**Added to the frame test (not model changes).**
- **Hair under the band (estimate).** The band's marks are scalp distances, but the band rides on the hair. A hair mat h thick over the roughly 1.8 rad from the hairline to Pz puts the Pz pod about 1.8 × h forward of the site: 4–7 mm for 2–4 mm of hair, about 1 mm at Fz. It repeats for the same patient unless the hair changes. Tape-check Fz and Pz (30% and 70% of nasion to inion) on short and thick hair. If Pz is off by more than 5 mm, zero the band at the vertex instead of Fpz, which halves the distance the error builds over.
- **Carriages:** none jumps its notch while nodding; the latch can be worked with one hand; the shoes slide on a PETG rail without binding (print the shoe gap at 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 mm and keep the one that slides).
- **Dial reading:** the PN521 knob has no numbers. Stick a paper scale on the toothed strap and read it at the housing edge, or the saved setting cannot be repeated.
- **Forehead pads:** the 32 × 25 mm gel pads at 27 mm centres leave about 2 mm between them with the 25 mm side across. Trim the foam borders so the gels cannot touch.

**From the same review, for the board (not changed yet):** input capacitors 1 nF down to 100–220 pF (a 30 kΩ pod against a 5 kΩ reference pad limits 50 Hz rejection to about 42 dB with 1 nF); 51 kΩ input resistors on any board a patient wears; 100 Ω in series on SCLK, MOSI and CS; gain 12 in the firmware; the ear sensor at 100 samples a second with its supply pair twisted.

## v6.3 (11 Nov): no Cz, two-window band
Decision D36 (Neuryo-Research, 11 Nov): no Cz channel in the product or the test kit, and the band needs no Cz window. Reasons: the session is rest only and every use of Cz in the library needs a task; at rest, the depression difference among single midline electrodes was at the frontmost site, not Cz (P202); each hair pod is one more contact that must pass before a session counts (D11). Channel 7 stays free on the board; the board is not changed.

**Band check (model estimate, `tools/band_fit.py`).** A sagittal beam model on the shared head scaled to nasion–inion 300–390 mm and front-back vs height ×0.92–1.08: band printed in this head's curve (324 mm), clamped at the front anchor, pulled by the tail, lying on a soft foundation (lining and hair), pushed off at each carriage by its pod spring. Every input is assumed, not measured: PETG E about 2.0 GPa (PA12 about 1.7), tail tension 4–10 N, pod spring 1–3 N, foundation stiffness. Results (largest values over all heads):

| Tail / spring | Lift at the Pz carriage, v6.2 → v6.3 | Lift at Fz | Worst Pz error incl. size rounding, v6.2 → v6.3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 N / 1 N | 1.9 → 1.9 mm | 0.1 → 0.2 mm | 5.1 → 4.4 mm |
| 4 N / 3 N | 2.8 → 2.6 mm | 0.5 → 0.4 mm | 6.4 → 4.9 mm |
| 6 N / 1 N | 1.2 → 1.4 mm | 0.1 → 0.2 mm | 4.4 → 4.0 mm |
| 6 N / 3 N | 1.7 → 1.9 mm | 0.2 → 0.3 mm | 4.9 → 4.3 mm |
| 10 N / 3 N | 0.8 → 1.1 mm | 0.1 → 0.2 mm | 4.0 → 3.7 mm |

- Removing the Cz window changes lift at the carriages by 0.3 mm or less in either direction. Fz stays within 2 mm in every case.
- The lift is at Pz on the largest heads (370–390 mm), and it is there in v6.2 too. It comes from the band being printed in a 324 mm head's curve and from the rear end of the band, not from the Cz change. Lift makes the Pz pod land forward, which adds to the hair shift (v6.2 estimate). On a bare scalp, rounding alone keeps Pz within 3 mm; with modelled lift the worst case is 3.7–4.9 mm.
- The model's best lever was printing the band in a mid-range head's curve: printed for a 345 mm head, Pz lift on the largest heads fell to 1.2–1.3 mm at 6 N / 3 N and the worst Pz error to 3.8 mm. Not changed here: it is a design call for the frame test (Neuryo-Research, Still open, 11 Nov).
- Peak bending stress in the band when fitted to the largest or smallest heads: about 7–10 MPa (sustained while worn). Creep of a pre-curved PETG band at that stress is not known (README gap).
- Not modelled: twist, side-to-side curvature, friction, the tail's rise into the cam lock, creep, print anisotropy.
