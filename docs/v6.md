Live: https://aryankalra404.github.io/Neuryo-Headset/designs/v6/

**What it is:** v4's midline band and spring carriages on a lighter frame. Electronics are unchanged from v4.5 / v5. Since v6.2 the product headset has 6 channels on the head (Fp1, Fp2, Fz, Pz, M2, eye; M1 reference, Fpz bias); the Cz pod is a test-kit part. See [v6.2](#v62-10-nov-fixes-from-the-design-review).

## Why (changes from v4 and v5)
- **Midline on the head, not over it.** A band lying on the head sets each pod's height from the scalp, so the spring only takes up hair. v5's rigid spine stood 29 mm off the head; the gap changed with head shape, which forced a long spring, wedge-cut carriages and an offset arch. Same principle as Zeto's FDA-cleared headset (semi-rigid bands with fixed Fz, Cz, Pz and a spring per electrode: Neuryo-Research C72, C80) and OpenBCI's spring holders (C71, C77). A light strip over the top on a headband was the most comfortable layout in Hairston 2014 (C82).
- **Small front anchor instead of the brow module.** The board is now above the left ear, so the front only has to hold the band's end and set its 0 mm mark over Fpz.
- **Head ring instead of the arch.** A band that lies on the head has to be held at both ends; the arch could not hold its front end. The front band and anchor do.
- **Cam lock above the dial.** v4 clamped the tail on the battery pod at the back; the battery is now above the right ear.

## Checks
- Model: windows and lining are real openings and the lead leaves each carriage through a slot. A collision check of the frame, band, anchor, carriages, pods and leads on the page (8 Nov) found no parts passing through each other; the only overlaps left are leads plugging into their snap heads and the pods.
- Not yet measured: the harness range (PN521), the front band's fit on small and large foreheads, the anchor size, spring force (kitchen scale). Patent: Zeto US 11,166,673 is probably still in force; check its claims before a product.

## v6.2 (10 Nov): fixes from the design review
A review of v6.1 against the Neuryo-Research library (10 Nov). Only the points that are clear problems with a clear fix are changed here. Nothing is printed yet, so sizes are still model estimates. Board changes from the same review are not made yet (listed at the end).

| What | v6.1 | v6.2 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carriage hold-down | Stood on the rails with a loose 2 mm pin in a size hole | Shoes that wrap the rails: over the top, down the outer edge, a 0.7 mm lip underneath. A printed latch in the right shoe clicks into a notch on the rail's outer edge | The spring pushes the pod down and the carriage up with the same 1–3 N. A pin in a hole stops sliding, not lifting, so the carriage would come off the band. No loose pin to drop in a clinic |
| Rails beside the windows | 7 × 1.8 mm | 7 × 3 mm | Two 1.8 mm rails beside a 64 mm window are the band's weakest point and bridge badly when the band is printed on its edge; the shoes also need an edge to grip. The band is still 1.8 mm everywhere else, so it still follows the head |
| Lining under the rails | 6 mm wide | 3.6 mm, on the window side | Leaves room for the shoe's lip under the rail's outer edge |
| Size marks | Holes in the middle of the right rail; numbers under the carriage's left foot | Notches on the right rail's outer edge; each number 6.5 mm in front of its position, so it shows at the pointer mark on the left shoe's front edge | The number was hidden under the carriage once it was set |
| Leads from the pods | On the band's left edge | 3 mm outboard of the edge, over the left shoe, in clips on the solid sections | The left shoe now slides along that edge |
| Cz pod | Third hair pod, channel 7 | Test kit only. Product headset: Fz and Pz. The band keeps the Cz window and notches, channel 7 stays free on the board | See below |
| Fitting step | Pin each carriage, then fit | Slide to the size number until it clicks; after the tail is locked, slide each carriage one click forward and back | The slide works the tip through the hair, as twisting does on an Ultracortex node (C71) |
| Try-on model | v6.1 | `neuryo-v6.glb` exported again from the v6.2 page | |

**Why Cz moved to the test kit.** Its two reasons do not apply to the session as planned: the P300 evidence (M10, C03) needs a task and the 5-minute session is rest only, and central cordance (K102, n=18) cannot be claimed from a 6 to 8 channel montage (D18). The 19+ channel wet-cap study answers whether Cz adds anything, and it does that without a Cz pod on the headset. Dropping it removes one contact that has to pass before a session counts (D11) and one pod to build. It is not proven useless: if the subset test says Cz adds, the pod goes back in the window that is already there.

**Kept as it was: two side pods of the same size.** The review suggested moving the battery into the left pod. That was wrong on balance: the board and the cell weigh about the same, so one each side balances the headset, and matching shells look right. Keep both. Twist the battery's two wires together along the rear band, since they carry the radio's current pulses next to the M2 lead.

**Checks (10 Nov, headless render).** No page errors in Product or Test kit mode. `tools/collide.js`: no new overlaps apart from each shoe meeting its own foot (one print) and the thicker rails meeting the band they are part of.

**Added to the frame test (not model changes).**
- **Hair under the band (estimate).** The band's marks are scalp distances, but the band rides on the hair. A hair mat h thick over the roughly 1.8 rad from the hairline to Pz puts the Pz pod about 1.8 × h forward of the site: 4–7 mm for 2–4 mm of hair, about 1 mm at Fz. It repeats for the same patient unless the hair changes. Tape-check Fz and Pz (30% and 70% of nasion to inion) on short and thick hair. If Pz is off by more than 5 mm, zero the band at the vertex instead of Fpz, which halves the distance the error builds over.
- **Carriages:** none jumps its notch while nodding; the latch can be worked with one hand; the shoes slide on a PETG rail without binding (print the shoe gap at 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 mm and keep the one that slides).
- **Dial reading:** the PN521 knob has no numbers. Stick a paper scale on the toothed strap and read it at the housing edge, or the saved setting cannot be repeated.
- **Forehead pads:** the 32 × 25 mm gel pads at 27 mm centres leave about 2 mm between them with the 25 mm side across. Trim the foam borders so the gels cannot touch.

**From the same review, for the board (not changed yet):** input capacitors 1 nF down to 100–220 pF (a 30 kΩ pod against a 5 kΩ reference pad limits 50 Hz rejection to about 42 dB with 1 nF); 51 kΩ input resistors on any board a patient wears; 100 Ω in series on SCLK, MOSI and CS; gain 12 in the firmware; the ear sensor at 100 samples a second with its supply pair twisted.
