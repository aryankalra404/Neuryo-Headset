## What changed from v2, and why

| Change | v2 | v3 | Why (decision · evidence) | How sure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference site | TP9, moved 15 mm back | **M1, mastoid bone** | The skin around the ear "including the mastoid bone" is described as hair free (P197); hair cover at TP9 is not reported; adhesive pads fail through hair (L37) | Medium |
| Second mastoid channel | TP10 | **M2** | Moves with the reference | Low to medium |
| EEG chip | ADS1299-6 | **8-channel ADS1299** | Already the part in the 29 Oct budget; same 64-pin package (X22). Frees ch 7 for ECG and ch 8 | Very sure |
| Gain | 24 (from v1) | **12** | ±187.5 mV at gain 24 cannot hold the ±300 mV offset tolerance or the IEC 300 mV test (X22, R17, C01); noise 0.16 vs 0.14 µVrms (D29) | Very sure |
| Reference routing | SRB1 | **SRB2, per channel** | SRB1 ties every N input together; with SRB2 one channel can run differential for ECG (X25) | High |
| Forehead contact | Hydrogel pad on a snap | **Flat Ag/AgCl + Ten20 paste in a snap socket** | Fp1 to Fpz is 26.9 mm on the 540 mm head; the budget's 32 × 25 mm pads would touch (L51). Wet Ag/AgCl is the reference standard. Small hydrogel discs stay a valid alternative | Medium |
| Electrode mounting | Fixed pods | **One 4 mm snap socket per site, swappable inserts** | Lets the team compare paste, semi-dry and dry on the same frame, as the test plan needs. Mixed contact types are fine with a high-impedance amplifier (L53, C03) | Engineering choice |
| Dry contacts | Not in the plan | **Test arms only (D30)** | Dry raised delta and theta against gel and lost more data (L19, L02); high impedance in warm rooms added slow noise (L46) | High-medium |
| Eye contact | Small gel pad | **Spare flat snap + paste, small ring** | The 32 × 25 mm budget pad would reach the lower eyelid at 20 mm below the eye. Uses a part already bought | Engineering choice |
| Power | MT3608 boost → 5 V LDO | **Cyton-style ±2.5 V, battery only** | Avoids a switching boost in front of the analog supply; matches the design being copied (X24) | Judgement call |
| Top arc | Fixed pods | **Sliding Fz and Pz mounts** | 10–20 sites scale with each head: nasion–inion 300–348 mm for 500–580 mm heads, so Fz moves ±7 mm and Pz ±17 mm | High (geometry) |
| Earlobe clip lead | Behind the ear | **In front of the ear** | Keeps it clear of the M1 pad | Engineering choice |
| Test kit | none | **ECG pads on ch 7, comparison inserts** | Validate earlobe PPG against ECG on our own sensor and site (D09) | High |

## On the page
- **Test kit** toggle: shows the ECG pads and the insert picker (forehead: paste or dry flat; hair: semi-dry, paste or dry spike). Test-only parts are labelled and listed under "Test kit only".
- **Confidence** toggle: colours every part as decided, needs testing, judgement call, or carried over from v1.
- **v2 sites** toggle: ghosts v2's behind-ear pads next to M1/M2.
- **Channels** tab: the 8-channel map; tap a row to see the part.
- **Size**: forehead spacing (27 mm) and the Fz/Pz slide ranges.
- **Board guide** and **Electrode build**: updated for J7 (ECG), SRB2, gain 12, the Cyton-style supply and the pod's lid stud.

## Budget notes (corrections to the 29 Oct review)
- **ADuM3160 is not needed for the prototype.** USB isolation only helps if the patient side has its own supply, and the TP4056 charger on the same USB-C port ties the board to the laptop's ground anyway (X23). Stream over Bluetooth; use USB only for charging and firmware with nothing attached to a person. Saves about ₹1,018.
- **Eye contact uses the spare TDE-211**, so no extra pad pack is needed.
- **Lead count:** 8 head contacts + 2 ECG = 10, exactly one FRI 10-pack, no spare.

## Model notes
- M1/M2 measured by `tools/mastoid.py`: halfway between ear-canal and earlobe height, 16 mm behind the back edge of the ear so a 25 mm pad clears it. Stored in `designs/shared/head/head.json` under `extra`.
- FRI does not publish TDE-211/212 dimensions. Disc (12 mm), stud (4 mm) and socket sizes are model estimates in the `DIM` object at the top of the page script: measure the real parts and update them.
- The front module, board outline and battery are still v1 shapes. The 8-input board is not designed yet; the board guide labels are v3 but part positions are estimates.
- ECG pad placement and lead routing are model estimates.

## Still to prove on real hardware
1. Contact on your own hair, oiled and washed: impedance every 15 min for 60 min, paste vs semi-dry vs dry spike at Fz/Pz, and paste vs dry flat at Fp1/Fp2.
2. Same-day and 1-week retest against the VIT wet cap (theta and alpha at Fp1, Fp2, Fz).
3. Earlobe PPG vs ECG on channel 7.
4. M1 vs TP9 on a few heads: pad adhesion and impedance through a session.
5. Noise floor with shorted inputs under 6 µVpp, on the Cyton-style supply.
