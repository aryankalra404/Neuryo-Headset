# v7 · simplified ring and fixed Fz (11 Nov 2026)

Live: https://aryankalra404.github.io/Neuryo-Headset/designs/v7/

**Status:** proposed lead design, from the 11 Nov design review of v6.3 (commit `de1e33d`) against the Neuryo-Research library (D01 to D36). v6 stays on the page as the adjustable-Fz alternative. Nothing here has been printed or measured: every size is a model estimate unless it says "computed from the model", and those are computed on the shared 540 mm head (nasion–inion 324 mm).

**What it is:** v6's idea, a midline band that lies on the head, made easier to build, slimmer and more repeatable to fit. Unchanged from v6.3: the montage (Fp1, Fp2, Fz, Pz, M2, eye; M1 reference; Fpz bias; no Cz, D36), the electrodes and pod internals, the rev B board layout and KiCad files, the firmware, the shared head and every decision in `00-decisions.md`.

## What changed from v6, and why

How sure: **Fix** (a clear problem with a clear fix), **Judgement** (a design call), **To test** (needs the frame test).

| ID | v6.3 | v7 | Why | How sure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Front band, then a rigid 98 mm pod, then the rear band: the pod shells carried the ring tension | One continuous ring strap; the pods ride on it (tunnel, 2 × M3, rear hole a 6 mm slot) | The pods acted as stiff links on the side of the head; stiff links whose fit changes with head size are where hot spots came from in the comparison studies v6 cites (C82). A hard-hat headband is one flexible loop with accessories clipped on. The slot lets the strap change curvature for wide and narrow heads without bending the pod | Fix |
| R2 | Nape U with a 60 mm level section and arms ending rigidly at the pods' back corners | PN521 rear section kept whole, pivoting on the ring strap's ends | The pods should not be the mounting points, and a rigid mount fixes the angle of the hook under the occiput, which differs between heads. Flat toothed straps bend round the head but not edgewise, so the loop stays one smooth curve through the gear (the v5 note stands) | Fix |
| R3 | None | Chin-strap tabs on the ring; strap in the test kit | Whatever presses the pods down pulls the ring up by about twice the band tension (`docs/v4.md`); at the 4–10 N of tail tension `tools/band_fit.py` assumes, that is 8–20 N of lift on a ring held by forehead friction and the hook. A chin strap is the standard answer on EEG caps; the tabs cost nothing | To test |
| P1 | Pods about 98 × 77 mm, uniform 22 mm off the skin | Flat face 15 mm off the skin, about 90 × 51 mm along the skin; JP1 and TP1 off; motion sensor lower | The board is flat and the head curves away under its ends, so v6's height was set by the ends while the centre was mostly air | Fix |
| M1 | Anchor notch lined up by eye with the centre mark of a strip placed with a paper template; strip first | Nasion gauge sets the band's zero; headset first, strip after from the anchor's pointer | The size notch is exact; the zero was the weak link. A physical stop repeats it every visit, and shows ride-up for free. Cost: three steps put the zero up to 1.5 mm from 0.1 × nasion–inion | Judgement |
| M2 | Fz carriage slides, five notches (1·2, 3·4, 5·6, 7·8, 9) | Fz fixed, one notch, no numbers; same carriage print as Pz | Fz's range is only 18 mm over 300–390 mm. The product needs the same spot on the same patient every visit (D03), which a fixed pod gives as well as a sliding one. 9 mm is under 3% of nasion–inion; neighbouring 10-10 sites are 10% apart. Removes four notches, labels that did not match Pz's, 12 mm of window and one setting. Cost: v6's "Fz within 2 mm of its site" no longer holds | Judgement |
| M3 | Tail travel "about 50 mm", tension by feel, spare end loose | Travel corrected to about 81 mm; pull tab with a zigzag stretch section and marks; free end folds back up to a keeper on the splice plate | The old figure was left over from the 300–348 mm range. The TPU tail is stiff (about 19 N per mm at 60 mm free length: EA / L with E about 26 MPa and 44 mm²), so a length mark does not set tension, and tension drives band lift and ring ride-up. Spare-end stowing only partly done (see Not done) | To test |
| M4 | None | Print aids in the Build tab: snip-off ribs in the Pz window, shoe gap test first, rinse the plunger sleeves | The rail over the Pz window is a 64 mm curved span that cannot bridge on its edge; the 0.7 mm shoe lip is the most fragile feature; dried saline in a sliding fit can jam | Fix |

## Computed numbers

### P1: what sets the pod height (computed from the v6.3 model)
Height of each part's top above the skin plane at the pod centre, along the pod's normal:

| Part | Top, mm |
|---|---|
| JP1 header with shunt | 16.6 |
| BMI270 breakout and lead (fixed inside the cover in v6) | 15.2 to 16.0 |
| TP1 ground test loop | 12.6 |
| J1 battery connector | 12.4 |
| XIAO USB-C | 11.8 |
| Board top face | 7.6 |

- JP1 and TP1 are "hand-solder if wanted" in `bom-hand.csv`. In v6's uniform shell, leaving JP1 off gained only 0.3 mm (J1 at the board's far end is next); with a flat face it gains 4 mm.
- With JP1 and TP1 not fitted and the BMI270 breakout on 2.1 mm foam tape over the board below the ADS1299 (its top now 12.2 mm, under J1), J1 sets the face: 12.4 + 0.5 mm air + 2 mm wall, so the face is at 15 mm.
- JP1 joins BIAS_OUT to the ADS1299's BIASIN pin. The bias drive to Fpz runs BIAS_OUT → R10 → BIAS and does not go through JP1, and the firmware leaves BIAS_MEAS off (CONFIG3 0xEE). If JP1 ever has to be closed: a flat wire link across its two holes (now in `pcb/README.md` step 5).
- Final pod (fit check, `tools/pod_fit.js`): every board part's bounding-box corners at least **2.51 mm** inside the cover's outer surface (needs 2.5: 0.5 air + 2 wall), set by the board's front-low corner; J1 2.60, the BMI270 lead 2.9, USB-C 3.2. Face 15.0 mm off the skin at the board centre. Footprint about **90 × 51 mm** along the skin through the pod centre (v6: 97 × 75); free space in the pod's board plane: 6.5 mm in front of the board's front edge (lead entry), 12 mm behind its back edge (battery plug, USB-C and switch slots). The footprint follows the board's projection, which drops toward its back end (lower and upper edges on lines, not a rectangle in angle).
- The side walls are taller where the skin falls away under the board's ends and corners. Expected.
- The battery cell in the right pod moved 4 mm up and 0.6 mm out (model estimate) to clear the strap's tunnel: 1.45 mm between the strap's top and the cell.

### R1: ring path (`tools/ring_check.js`, final keys in the page code)
- Strap's lower edge to the top of the ear: at least **18.7 mm** (needs 8).
- Strap to the forehead strip: at least **3.9 mm** (needs 3), at about 60° each side.
- Strap's top to the board's underside (and its bottom-side parts) under the left pod: at least **2.26 mm** (needs 0.5 with 1 mm lining and 0.5 mm air); the strap fits under the board's lower third.
- No kink: tightest bend of the centre line about 24 mm radius (where the strap descends behind the pod into the nape section, on the head, so it includes the head's own curvature); the turn across each pivot is 1.3°, the same as between neighbouring samples.
- Flat outline (model estimate): about **150 × 150 mm** in its best-fit plane, inside a 220 × 220 mm bed. It is not flat: the centre line drops about 43 mm from the forehead to the pivots and leaves the best-fit plane by up to 8 mm, so printed on its edge it needs supports under the low points (Still open).

### M3: tail travel (`tools/tail_travel.js`)
The band's rigid part ends **251.5 mm** behind the band's zero (Fpz) for every head (computed from the v6.3 model). The cam lock sits near the inion, 0.895 × nasion–inion behind the zero on this head (290 mm). Free tail:

| Nasion–inion | Free tail |
|---|---|
| 300 mm | 17 mm |
| 324 mm (this head) | 39 mm |
| 390 mm | 98 mm |

- **Travel about 81 mm** (v6 said about 50 mm, from the old 300–348 mm range). Spec estimate was 82 mm (23 to 105 mm with the lock at exactly 0.9 × NI); the model puts the lock at 0.895 × NI along the band.
- At 300 mm the splice plate and the lock's jaw do not meet: about 3 mm between them (plate 16 mm long, jaw 12 mm, model).
- Spare end on small heads: up to about 81 mm of tail comes out of the lock at 300 mm, plus the 45 mm pull tab.

### M2: Fz offset (fit panel)
Fz is fixed for a 345 mm head. Sign: + means the pod sits behind (further from the nasion than) its 10-20 site.

| Nasion–inion | Pod term 0.2 × (345 − NI) | Gauge step (zero − 0.1 × NI) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 mm (size 1, gauge 1-3) | +9.0 | +1.5 | +10.5 |
| 345 mm (size 5, gauge 4-6) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 390 mm (size 9, gauge 7-9) | −9.0 | −1.5 | −10.5 |

The fit panel shows both terms. The spec's ±9 mm is the pod term; with the gauge's 1.5 mm steps the extremes are ±10.5 mm.

### Band check (`tools/band_fit_v7.py`, model estimate)
`tools/band_fit.py` copied with v7's band: one 20 mm Fz window with the pod fixed at 0.2 × 345 mm, and the zero from the gauge (three steps) instead of exactly 0.1 × NI. Same assumptions as v6.3 (PETG E about 2.0 GPa, foundation 3 N/mm², all assumed). Largest values over 300–390 mm and front-back vs height ×0.92–1.08:

| Tail / spring | Lift at Pz, v6.3 → v7 | Lift at Fz, v6.3 → v7 | Worst Fz error, v6.3 → v7 | Worst Pz error incl. rounding, v6.3 → v7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 N / 1 N | 1.9 → 2.1 mm | 0.2 → 0.3 mm | 2.0 → 10.6 mm | 4.4 → 5.9 mm |
| 4 N / 3 N | 2.6 → 2.8 mm | 0.4 → 0.6 mm | 2.0 → 10.6 mm | 4.9 → 6.5 mm |
| 6 N / 1 N | 1.4 → 1.5 mm | 0.2 → 0.3 mm | 2.0 → 10.6 mm | 4.0 → 5.5 mm |
| 6 N / 3 N | 1.9 → 2.0 mm | 0.3 → 0.3 mm | 2.0 → 10.6 mm | 4.3 → 5.8 mm |
| 10 N / 3 N | 1.1 → 1.2 mm | 0.2 → 0.3 mm | 2.0 → 10.6 mm | 3.7 → 5.2 mm |

- The shorter Fz window and the longer solid section (95 mm, was 90) change lift by 0.2 mm or less. Lift is still at Pz on the largest heads.
- Fz error is the fixed pod (±9 mm) plus the gauge step (±1.5 mm), by design. It repeats for the same patient.
- Pz error grows by 1.5 mm: that is the gauge's step. v6.3's model assumed the template put the zero exactly at 0.1 × NI, which a sticker lined up by eye may not do; the frame test's five-refit check is what compares the two.
- Printing the band in a 345 mm head's curve (still open from v6.3) cuts Pz lift on the largest heads to 0.7–1.9 mm with v7's band too.

## Frame test additions
Add to the existing list:
- Ride-up: after tightening the tail, does the gauge foot still sit in the nasion? Repeat after 5 minutes and after nodding. With and without the chin strap.
- Tail tension with a luggage scale at the pull tab, against band lift at Pz (feeler gauge) and ride-up.
- Refit the same person five times: tape-check Fz and Pz each time. This is the number that matters (D03).
- Pods on the strap: do they rock or leave a gap at the top edge on narrow and wide heads?
- Nape pivot: does the hook seat under the occiput on its own?
- Long hair, a low ponytail and a braid: where do they go relative to the nape strap and the tail?
- Pull-tab coupons: hang 400 g and 600 g; keep the beam width (1.6, 2.0, 2.4 mm at 2 mm thick) that stretches about 10–15 mm at 5 N.
- Ring strap print: does the strap print on its edge with supports under its low points, and does it go on without forcing it edgewise (see Still open)?

## Still open
- Nothing printed: every size in this file is a model estimate on one head.
- The ring strap is not flat (up to 8 mm out of its best-fit plane). Printed on its edge it needs supports; if that is poor, try printing it in two halves joined under the anchor, or use the PN521's own headband as the ring if it is the right width at the forehead (a build note in R1).
- The pull tab's stretch marks are untested, and TPU relaxes under load: the marks may drift with use. Check them against weights at the start of each session day until the frame test says how fast they drift.
- The pods ride on the strap with 2 × M3 each: whether they rock on narrow and wide heads (frame test).
- The nape section's real travel through the PN521 housing (each toothed strap needs about 40 mm for 500–580 mm heads) is on the "measure when it arrives" list.
- The gauge's zero steps add up to 1.5 mm at Pz (band check above). A continuous gauge (a sliding stop with a scale) would remove that at the cost of one more setting.
- Fixed Fz gives up v6's "within 2 mm" at Fz. If the team wants absolute 10-20 accuracy for publication, v6's Fz window goes back in.
- Printing the band in a 345 mm head's curve (from v6.3).
- Lengthen the Pz window's front end by about 2 mm: at size 1 the pod's tip collar touches it (check 6). Same in v6.3; not changed here because the spec keeps Pz as v6.3.

## Not done
- **M3, stowing the spare end by folding it back up over the tail.** Clearly wrong in the model on small heads. The tail past the lock is the spare end plus the 45 mm pull tab: about 142 mm minus the free tail. Folded back up over the tail it reaches the Pz carriage's rear edge on every head under about 341 mm nasion–inion (sizes 1–4): on this head (324 mm) about 104 mm of tail would fold back where there is about 83 mm from the lock to the carriage; at 300 mm, about 126 mm into about 76 mm (lock at 0.895 × NI, Pz at 0.6 × NI, carriage radius 12.2 mm, tail 97.5 + 45 mm past the rigid end). The page draws the keeper on the splice plate and the pull tab folded back over the tail as on the largest heads (no spare before the tab), and says so on the part card. Options for the team: two tail lengths (sizes 1–4 and 5–9, spliced per headset, not per patient); a shorter tab with the spare end Z-folded under the keeper; or a spare end that exits the lock downward under a keeper on the nape strap beside the dial (has to stay clear of the knob).
- **M5, L1, X1** (priority 3 and 4, optional): left for v7.1. M5 first.
