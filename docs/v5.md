# v5 · 7 Nov arch concept

**Status:** concept, to test against v4. v4 (v4.5) stays the build plan until the v5 mock-up passes the frame test below.

**Open it:** [designs/v5/](https://aryankalra404.github.io/Neuryo-Headset/designs/v5/)

**Source:** the "Neuryo headset concept study" doc (7 Nov 2026): competitor research, mechanics check and the design checklist. Electronics, channels, pods, strip and session are unchanged from [v4](v4.md).

## Why
The v4.5 brow module had to grow to about 140 × 65 mm and 29.5 mm off the forehead to hold the 70 × 50 mm board. It looks bulky and comes close to the eyes on small foreheads. v5 keeps the forehead bare apart from the strip.

## What changed from v4
| | v4 (v4.5) | v5 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame | Ratchet halo + printed midline band + cam lock | Spring arch over Cz + temple arms + rear band with a dial | Forehead bare; the arch lies on the T7–Cz–T8 line, so the ears place Cz. Muse and Emotiv put electronics behind or above the ears |
| Main board | Brow module, about 29.5 mm off the forehead | Left pod above the ear (az 100°, el 34° on the head model), about 20.5 mm off the skin | Forehead radius is about 67 mm, so a flat 70 mm board lifts about 10 mm at its ends there; the side of the head is flatter. M1, eye pad and ear clip are on the left |
| Battery | Back pod over the ratchet | Right pod above the ear, same shell | Balances the left pod, about 32 g vs 30 g (estimates) |
| Hold-down | Ratchet all round | Rear hook under the occiput, tightened by a dial | See the mechanics check below |
| Fz, Pz | Carriages on the band, pinned from Fpz | Carriages under a bar pivoting at Cz; Cz fixed under the arch | Fz and Pz share the force; 5 holes 4 mm apart per pod, two sizes each, within 2 mm |
| Forehead | Brow module over the strip | Strip only; tail plugs into a socket under the left temple pad | No box on the forehead |
| Bought harness | Karam PN521 | Not needed (a hard-hat knob can be reused for the dial) | |

## Mechanics check (estimates, from the concept study)
- **Pod force:** 1–1.5 N each. Three pods at 1 N push the headset up about 3 N.
- **What holds it down without the hook:** weight about 1.3 N, and the pods clamping on skin that faces only about 20% downward add about 0.8 N. Not enough without relying on friction.
- **Rear hook:** below the inion the skin faces 42–49% downward (measured on this head model), so 3 N on the dial adds about 1.4 N of hold-down. At 1.5 N per pod the hook carries about 5 N, about 7 kPa on a 40 × 20 mm pad.
- **Temple pads** stop the rear band pulling the headset backward.
- **Bar:** 78 mm cantilever each side of Cz. 12 × 5 mm PETG (about 2 GPa assumed), or 12 mm wide with a 1.2 mm steel strip inside, keeps Fz and Pz within ±0.2 N of each other at 1 N with a spring of about 0.33 N/mm.
- **Arch sliders:** about ±15 mm per side for 500–580 mm heads (this head's 355 mm ear-to-ear arc scaled with circumference).

## Wiring
- Fz, Cz, Pz: along the bar to Cz, then inside the arch down into the left pod (J1).
- M1 and the ear clip: straight up into the left pod (J2, J6).
- M2, battery and the right ECG lead: up into the right pod and over the arch (J3, J5, J7).
- Strip tail (Fp1, Fpz, Fp2) and eye lead: socket under the left temple pad, then along the left temple arm (J2, J3). About 12–20 cm.
- Bias leaves on J2 through the strip's tail. The antenna sits on the pod's outer face, away from the steel arch.

## Frame test before electronics
Mock up the arch from the spring band of cheap over-ear headphones. Wear it on 10 heads from 500 to 580 mm, with thick, oiled and short hair, braids, glasses and a hearing aid if possible. Nod and turn for 12 minutes. Pass if each pod keeps its force on a kitchen scale, the bar does not bend, the arch top stays within 5 mm of the size tape's Cz mark and nobody reports a sore spot.
