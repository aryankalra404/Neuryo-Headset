# v5 · 7 Nov trial: board in a side box

**Status:** trial. v4 stays the current plan; v5 tries one change, where the main board goes. Everything not listed here is v4.4 unchanged ([v4.md](v4.md)).

**Page:** https://aryankalra404.github.io/Neuryo-Headset/designs/v5/

## Why v5 exists
v4 put a 60 × 32 mm board inside the 15 mm brow module. That size was a model estimate. The real board is rev A ([pcb/](../pcb/README.md), KiCad ERC and DRC clean, not yet built): 100 × 80 mm, with an ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 plugged on two sockets on top. A careful rev B is aimed at about 90 × 60 mm, and with the DevKit it stands about 21 mm tall. That does not fit the brow module.

The back of the head was checked first and does not work either. The ratchet knob sits at halo level in the middle of the back, and the band's splice, TPU tail and cam lock sit just above it (the splice is about 50 mm above the halo on this head, lower on small heads). A box big enough for the board would cover the knob or hit the band.

The left side above the halo is free: no pods, no band. So v5 puts the board in a printed box there.

## Rev B: what changes from rev A
- BMI270 chip on the board instead of the MPU-6050 module header (J9).
- Earlobe heart clip on I²C bus 0 (J7), test-kit forehead window on bus 1 (J8).
- Channel labels: Cz on channel 7, ECG on channel 8 (rev A: ECG on 7). J4 already routes all 8 inputs, so this is labels and firmware.
- Power budget written for the 1000 mAh cell (rev A assumed 2500 mAh).
- Outline: target 90 × 60 mm, keeping the DevKit on sockets, single-sided assembly and the 4-layer stack-up. If it does not route cleanly at that size, it stays at 100 × 80 mm and the box grows to about 114 × 92 mm.
- Analog section, ±2.5 V supply, charger, protection and the ESP32 pin map: unchanged from rev A.
- Before ordering: an ECE professor's review, caliper check of the DevKit header spacing, battery polarity check (rev A's REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md).

Neuryo's top has the band and the Cz pod and its back has the ratchet and the band lock, so the free spot is the side, as on the EPOC.

## Weight and balance (estimates, to check on the frame test)
- Side box with board: about 60 g on the left. Battery pod at the back: about 25 g. Brow module: lighter than v4.
- The halo is snug all round, and the session is 5 minutes upright. Check on the frame test that the halo does not tip toward the left; if it does, move the battery from the back pod to a small right-side pod to balance.
