## What changed from v1, and why

| Change | v1 | v2 | Why (decision · evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forehead sites | AF7, AF8 | **Fp1, Fp2** | The best-supported signal for tracking treatment is the week-1 drop in prefrontal theta at Fp1/Fp2 (D13 · K79, K80, K85). AF7/AF8 had no reliability or psychiatric evidence, and the Muse layout that uses them performed poorly at the front and sides (D24 · C15). |
| Channel count | 4 | **6** | Adds TP10 as its own channel and an eye channel; keeps Pz for P300 (D08). The 4-channel fallback is Fp1, Fp2, Fz, VEOG. |
| Reference | TP9 + TP10 joined in hardware | **TP9 alone**; linked mastoids only in software | Joining the ears in hardware bridges the two hemispheres (D06 · P04). Average or REST references need far more channels (D25 · P04). |
| Eye channel | none | **VEOG below the left eye** | Fp1/Fp2 pick up the most blink and eye-movement noise. Removing it needs a reference eye channel, because ICA can't run on so few channels (D14, D26 · P12, P14). |
| Heartbeat noise | not handled | **TP10 channel gives a heartbeat reference** | Ear and mastoid electrodes pick up the heartbeat strongly (D16 · P111, P112). |
| Electrodes | Saline sponge everywhere | **Hydrogel on bare forehead skin; semi-dry reservoir pods only in hair; sticky gel behind the ears and below the eye** | Plain sponges dried within about 20 minutes (D21 · L37). Semi-dry stayed under 40 kΩ for 5 h through hair (P29). Ag/AgCl at every site had the lowest drift (D07 · L10). |
| Heart sensor | Forehead | **Earlobe clip** | Earlobe pulse readings agreed best with ECG; there is no evidence for the forehead (D22 · X08, M09). Readings are labelled pulse-rate variability, not HRV (D09). |
| Contact check | Lead-off at 31.2 Hz all the time | **Between blocks only** | The built-in test signal sits inside the EEG band (D27 · X01). |
| EEG chip | ADS1299-4 | **ADS1299-6** | Needed for six channels. |
| Fit | Elastic strap and neoprene band | **Fitted frame, 500 to 580 mm** | Re-placement moved electrodes 3 mm with a fitted cap against 9.8 mm with straps; repeatability is the top requirement (D03, D19 · P186, P190). |
| Mannequin | Formula head; sites set by angle | **Realistic head (MakeHuman, CC0), 540 mm, every site measured with the 10-20 rules** | So electrode, ear and earlobe positions are believable. See [head.md](head.md). |

## Confidence
- **High:** Fp1/Fp2, Ag/AgCl, fitted frame, earlobe PPG, no hardware-linked mastoids.
- **Medium:** Fz, Pz, the eye channel, TP9 as reference, hydrogel on the forehead.
- **Judgement calls:** exactly 6 channels, the TP10 channel, bias at Fpz, semi-dry vs dry multipin in hair, IMU placement.

## Still to prove on real hardware
1. Same-day and 1-week test-retest against a wet cap.
2. Impedance over 60 minutes on Indian hair, oiled and washed (decides the forehead and hair-site electrode type).
3. 3-electrode theta cordance against full-cap cordance (D18).
4. Earlobe PPG against ECG.

## Model notes
- TP9 and TP10 sit 15 mm behind the standard 10-10 point so the pads clear this mannequin's ear.
- The front module, board layout and battery are v1's and have not been redesigned for six channels.
- Clip, pad and lead sizes are model estimates.
